Aquifer Wells

Four wells draw from the same underground aquifer. Two are shallow and two are deep, each with a small or a large pump. Raise or lower the water table and watch it settle slowly to the new level. A well only delivers water while the water table is above its intake, and a running pump pulls the water down around its own well.

The small pumps deliver up to 4 L/s and the large pumps up to 10 L/s. A pump runs at full rate while the water stays above the top of its intake screen, slows as the water drops past the screen, and runs dry once the water is below it. The larger the flow, the deeper the dip it pulls in the water table around its well. Each pipe's dots, its flow meter, and the width of its outflow all grow with the flow. Tap a pump to switch it on or off.
