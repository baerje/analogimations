Aquifer Wells

Four wells draw from the same underground aquifer. Two are shallow and two are deep, each with a low-capacity or a high-capacity pump. Raise or lower the water table and watch it settle slowly to the new level. A well only delivers water while the water table is above its intake, and a running pump pulls the water down around its own well.

The low-capacity pumps deliver up to 4 L/s and the high-capacity pumps up to 10 L/s. The pumps sit in the pool and everything they pump sprays out of the top like a fountain and falls back into it. The pool which drains at a rate that rises with its level, so it settles fuller the more water comes in. A pump runs at full rate while the water stays above the top of its intake screen, slows as the water drops past the screen, and runs dry once the water is below it. The larger the flow, the deeper the dip it pulls in the water table around its well. Each pump's flow shows in the dots rising up its pipe, its flow meter, and the height and width of its fountain. Tap a pump to switch it on or off.
